I’m Mohammad Mansib Newaz, a developer based in Bangladesh. My focus is practical AI and automation: tools that fit a real task and are understandable to the people using them.

From model to usable interface

My public work spans computer vision research and business workflow interfaces. The Traffic Sign Detection project connects training and inference work to a Gradio interface; the CRM and manufacturing projects explore how people navigate operational information.

I care about the space between a working prototype and a useful tool: clear requirements, realistic examples, visible error states, and documentation that helps someone else continue the work.

How I approach a project

Start with the existing workflow. Identify the part worth improving. Make the smallest useful version, review its limitations, and agree on what is needed before a wider rollout.

AI output needs evaluation. Interfaces need to work on real devices. An integration needs a sensible response when a service is unavailable. These are part of the scope from the start.
