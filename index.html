04 / Before we start

A few practical details.

What makes a good first project?

One recurring task with clear inputs, a person who can review the output, and a manageable set of tools. We can start with a prototype before agreeing on a larger rollout.

What if AI is not the right fit?

A simple integration or rule-based workflow may be enough. The scope should follow the problem, the data, and the cost of mistakes.

How are timing and cost agreed?

After reviewing the workflow, access requirements, and expected output, we can agree on a scope and estimate. API and hosting costs should be discussed before implementation.

What should I send in the brief?

A short description of the workflow, the tools involved, and your preferred timeframe. Use fictional examples in the first message; please leave out credentials and sensitive customer data.
