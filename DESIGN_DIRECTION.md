# Obsidian Field Manual

## Vibe spec

**Thesis:** An AI-engineering portfolio should feel like a confident technical field manual: calm, precise, and visibly engineered rather than a generic neon SaaS template.

- **Reference:** a black-bound engineering notebook beside an illuminated lab instrument.
- **Colour system:** ink black `#0b0f12`, graphite `#111a1d`, signal mint `#6ff7d9`, safety orange `#ff9f55`, and cool sky `#8ecfff`. Mint is reserved for the primary path; orange calls out the human decision points. Both themes retain strong text contrast.
- **Type:** Cabinet Grotesk carries the display voice; General Sans keeps reading effortless; JetBrains Mono is reserved for technical labels and metrics.
- **Composition:** large, editorial headline blocks face a structured instrument panel. Thin rules, compact labels, and asymmetric glows create a measured visual rhythm.
- **Intentional collision:** editorial restraint appears in the typography, spacing, and field-manual labels; live instrumentation appears in the orbit, progress rail, active navigation, and signal accents.
- **Wildcard:** the scroll rail behaves like a calibration line, showing a visitor's position through the portfolio without hijacking browser scrolling.
- **Motion:** deliberate reveal, section orientation, and button feedback only. Movement is disabled for reduced-motion preferences.

## Copy strategy

- **Audience:** product teams and hiring managers evaluating an AI engineer.
- **Value proposition:** turn computer-vision and LLM prototypes into production systems with reliable interfaces and clear technical decisions.
- **Hero:** “AI systems that make it past the demo.”
- **Supporting copy:** computer vision, LLM/RAG, and full-stack systems that move from prototype to production.
- **Primary action:** “Plan a build” continues the hero story by starting a scoped collaboration conversation.
- **Likely hesitations:**
  1. *Can this move beyond a model demo?* The capabilities section names APIs, dashboards, integrations, deployment, and security.
  2. *Is there evidence of applied work?* The selected-work section leads with concrete portfolio case studies and avoids invented social proof.
  3. *Will a conversation be clear and low-friction?* The contact CTA specifies the possible next step and the command palette makes navigation quick.

## Section narrative

Hero → capabilities → selected case studies → core stack → current engineering focus → contact. Every section either clarifies delivery capability or reduces friction on the next step.
